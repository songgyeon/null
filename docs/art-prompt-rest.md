# 이재언 — 남은 25장

35장 중 10장은 받았다(프로필·미술관·work·bottle·chart·mug·treat·car·
laundry·record). 여기 있는 것은 **아직 안 받은 25장**이다. 번호를 1부터
다시 매겼다. 전체 목록은 `art-prompt-all.md`에 있다.

새 프로필(약장 앞 정면 초상)을 레퍼런스로 첨부하고 통째로 붙여넣는다.

---

```
첨부한 이미지가 이 인물의 레퍼런스다. 아래 25장을 순서대로 그려라.
인물이 나오는 장은 전부 이 사람, 이 얼굴, 이 안경, 이 화풍이어야 한다.
STYLE LOCK이 A와 B 두 개 있으니 각 번호에 적힌 쪽을 쓴다.
한 번에 한 장씩, 각 장 앞에 파일명을 적어라. 내가 "다음"이라고 하면
다음 번호로 간다.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
CHARACTER LOCK — 인물이 나오는 장 전부 동일
═══════════════════════════════════════════

Korean man, 29 years old, school nurse. Same face in every image as the
attached reference.

FACE — clean-cut and handsome, reads clearly as late twenties. Smooth
full mid-face, no hollowing under the cheekbones, no nasolabial folds,
no forehead lines, no crow's feet. Firm but not heavy jaw, softly
rounded chin. Calm level eyes, single-fold lids, only the faintest
shadow beneath them — quiet tiredness, not age. Straight dark brows.
Closed relaxed mouth, neutral corners. Default expression: tired and
composed, never smiling.

GLASSES — thin round silver wire-frame glasses, worn in every image
where his face is visible. Same frame every time.

SKIN — pale. Fair, cool-toned, bloodless but clean and well-kept.
Smooth and even with a faint sheen. Lips keep a trace of natural
colour. No sallow or grey cast, no visible pores, no dry matte texture.

HANDS — smooth backs, no raised veins or standing tendons.

HAIR — black, soft-textured, medium length, parted loosely with strands
falling over the brow and past the ear.

CLOTHING — a pressed pale grey shirt under a navy fine-knit cardigan,
sometimes a charcoal knit vest instead. Achromatic, immaculately
ironed. At school he wears a lanyard ID card reading "이재언 /
보건교사". At home, the same shirt without the cardigan. Winter
throughout.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STYLE LOCK A — 인물이 나오는 장
═══════════════════════════════════════════

Semi-realistic Korean illustration, cinematic but softly painted.
Smooth gradient shading with minimal hard facial shadow modeling,
softly painted edges. Cool muted winter palette — navy, slate, dusty
violet, with one warm light source per frame. Low contrast on the face:
light falls across the room, not across his cheek. Shallow depth of
field, grounded everyday interiors with real clutter. Illustrative,
not photographic.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STYLE LOCK B — 인물이 없는 장
═══════════════════════════════════════════

Photograph, not illustration. Handheld phone snapshot, available light
only, slight grain and soft focus falloff. Ordinary and unstyled — the
frame of someone who took it without thinking. Cold muted colour.
NO PERSON anywhere in frame.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
NEGATIVE — 25장 전부
═══════════════════════════════════════════

photorealistic skin texture, visible pores, nasolabial folds, laugh
lines, forehead wrinkles, crow's feet, hollow cheeks, gaunt, heavy jaw,
jowls, stubble, five o'clock shadow, deep dark circles, harsh side key
light, dramatic chiaroscuro, matte dry skin, veiny hands, prominent
tendons, middle-aged, 40s, weathered, sallow, grey complexion, sickly,
ashen, colourless lips, gothic vampire, smiling, open mouth, cigarette

═══════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT LIST — 25장
═══════════════════════════════════════════

── A. 프로필 배경 (4장) · STYLE B · 인물 없음 ──
   CHARACTER LOCK은 쓰지 않는다. 넷 다 사람이 한 명도 없다.
   방금 나갔거나 곧 올 자리만 찍는다. 관계가 깊어질수록 이 순서다.

1. jaeeon-landing — 3:4 세로 · 사진
Apartment stairwell landing in the evening, no person. Concrete steps,
painted steel handrail, a frosted window throwing cool light, one warm
bulb above. Nobody there.

2. jaeeon-lobby — 3:4 세로 · 사진
Building lobby at night, no person. Glass walls with faint
reflections, floor lit from overhead, empty seating. Nobody there.

3. jaeeon-drive — 3:4 세로 · 사진
Interior of a parked car at night seen from the passenger seat, no
person. Rain beading on the windshield, a red traffic light and
apartment windows blurred beyond, dark dashboard, a paper cup of
coffee in the console holder.

4. jaeeon-kitchen — 3:4 세로 · ★ 마지막 단계 · 사진
Home kitchen late at night, no person. On the table, TWO washed rice
bowls turned upside down on a folded cloth to dry, and one dark mug
with coffee still in it. Under-cabinet light warm, blinds cool blue
with the city beyond. The two bowls must be clearly visible and are
the point of the picture.

── B. 보건실과 학교 (8장) · STYLE A · 전부 1:1 ──
   폰으로 찍은 느낌. [셀카]는 본인이 든 폰으로 찍은 각도다.

5. jaeeon-sink
Nurse's office sink, washing his hands and rolling a sleeve up the
forearm. Close, hands and forearms in focus, his face only partly in
frame. Daylight.

6. jaeeon-care [셀카]
Holding a strip of medicine, a bottle of water and a folded blanket
gathered against his chest in both arms. Taken by himself, phone
raised. Nurse's office, daylight.

7. jaeeon-bandage
Winding a bandage around a student's forearm. Close on both pairs of
hands, the student only partly in frame. Nurse's office. His face calm
and half-lowered.

8. jaeeon-cabinet
Restocking a medicine cabinet, boxes lined up square on the shelf, one
in his hand. Nurse's office, daylight.

9. jaeeon-door
The corridor just outside the nurse's office door, leafing through
documents while walking. Daylight through the corridor windows.

10. jaeeon-corridor
School corridor at night, holding a stack of documents, dark outside
the windows. Fluorescent ceiling light, empty hallway.

11. jaeeon-classroom
A classroom during the school day, standing near the desks, winter
light through tall windows. Empty or nearly empty room.

12. jaeeon-rooftop
School rooftop, alone, wind in his hair, hands in his pockets. He is
NOT smoking and there is no cigarette anywhere in frame. Overcast
winter sky.

── C. 집과 바깥 (7장) · STYLE A · 전부 1:1 ──

13. jaeeon-driveseat
Parked car at dusk, seen from the driver's seat, a paper cup in one
hand resting on the wheel. He has not gone home yet and is not going
to say so.

14. jaeeon-market [셀카]
Grocery aisle, a basket in his hand holding food enough for TWO
people. Taken by himself under flat supermarket light.

15. jaeeon-cook [셀카]
Home kitchen, a table set with rice and soup laid for TWO, two bowls.
Taken by himself, phone raised, he is half in frame. Warm evening
kitchen light.

16. jaeeon-evening
At home after work, sitting, the day finished. No cardigan, shirt
sleeves rolled. Low warm lamp, dark window. Quiet.

17. jaeeon-curtain
At home, standing beside the curtain in profile, doing nothing in
particular, one hand near the fabric. Soft window light.

18. jaeeon-back
At home, turned away toward the window — HIS FACE IS NOT VISIBLE AT
ALL, seen from behind. On the table beside him a mug has gone cold.
Grey daylight.

19. jaeeon-shelf
Library stacks, pulling a book from a shelf. Seen from behind — HIS
FACE IS NOT VISIBLE AT ALL. The book is a plain paperback of Camus's
"The Stranger". Warm shelf-level light.

── D. 히든 (5장) · 전부 1:1 ────────────────
   20~23은 인물이 없다 · STYLE B.  24만 STYLE A다.

20. jaeeon-bag — 사진 · 인물 없음
Still life, top-down. The contents of a man's shoulder bag laid out in
one neat row on a flat surface: a folded timetable, a pen, a blister
strip of painkillers, keys, a plain handkerchief pressed square.
Everything aligned, nothing personal. Soft even light.

21. jaeeon-room — 사진 · 인물 없음
An empty bedroom, no person. Bed made tight with square corners,
nothing on the floor, one shelf of books, a single chair. Almost
nothing personal on display. Cold morning light.

22. jaeeon-playlist — 사진 · 인물 없음
Still life. An old portable music player screen, or a handwritten
track list on worn paper beside it. The same short list for years,
edges softened by handling. Close, soft light.

23. jaeeon-ticket — 사진 · 인물 없음
Still life. A single kept ticket stub on a dark surface, paper faded
and creased once down the middle, the print half worn away. Nothing
else in frame. Close, soft light.

24. jaeeon-yearbook — STYLE A · ★ 여기만 나이가 다르다
A page of an old school yearbook photographed close, showing one
formal graduation ID portrait of the SAME man at NINETEEN. Override
the age in CHARACTER LOCK to 19 and keep everything else: jaw a shade
softer, same pale cool skin, same calm level eyes, NO GLASSES yet,
hair neater and shorter, black school uniform with a stiff collar,
plain pale grey studio backdrop, blank unsmiling expression. Slight
print grain and a faint paper sheen over the page.

── E. 편의점 (1장) · STYLE A · 1:1 ─────────

25. jaeeon-conv
Convenience store at night, standing at the counter, holding a card
out to pay. A small bag of snacks on the counter that is plainly not
for him. Flat white store light, shelves and a dark window behind. He
is not looking at the camera.
```

## 헷갈리기 쉬운 것 넷

- **4번 부엌** — 그릇 두 개가 이 사람의 결말이다. 1인분을 계량하던
  사람한테 2인분이 손에 붙었다는 게 그 사진이다.
- **12번 옥상** — 담배가 한 개비도 들어가면 안 된다. 금연 5년째다.
- **18·19번** — 얼굴이 안 나오는 것이 이 두 장의 요점이다.
- **24번 졸업사진** — 여기만 열아홉이고, 여기만 안경이 없다.
